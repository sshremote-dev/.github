# Support

- Questions and ideas: open a discussion or an issue in [sshremote](https://github.com/sshremote-dev/sshremote/issues).
- Bugs: open an issue with your version (`sshremote --version`), platform and what happened.
- Security problems: report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

The [docs](https://sshremote.dev/docs.html) answer most questions about setting it up.
